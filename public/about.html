Our Story

Affordability and quality for every Nigerian

Ape-X Gym was founded in 2015 in Lagos, Nigeria. In the beginning, it operated much like every other gym in the city – catering to a small percentage of fitness enthusiasts who already worked out regularly.

Our founders quickly recognised that there was a greater opportunity to serve a much larger segment of the population. They asked themselves, "Why does 80% of Nigerians not belong to a gym?"

The answer? First‑time and casual gym users didn't like the intimidating "look at me" attitudes found in typical gyms, and they didn't want to pay a fortune just to try fitness.

So they completely changed the gym environment – both in attitude and format – creating the welcoming, low‑cost model that is now revolutionising the fitness industry in Nigeria. Ape-X Gym became known as "Your Comfort Zone™" – a friendly community where everyone feels comfortable, regardless of their fitness level.

From Lagos to the Nation

Today, Ape-X Gym has become one of the fastest‑growing fitness chains in Nigeria, with locations in Lagos, Abuja, Port Harcourt, Ibadan, and beyond. We've continued to spread our unique mission of enhancing people's lives by providing an affordable, high‑quality fitness experience in a welcoming, non‑intimidating environment.

Our expansion is driven by one simple belief: every Nigerian deserves access to a gym where they feel they belong.
